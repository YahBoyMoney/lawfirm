Quality lead screen

What makes the first review useful.

The best first message is not long. It is organized. The goal is to give the firm enough to check urgency, fit, proof, and whether the matter can realistically move.

Good-fit details to include

  • Dates, locations, and the county or court if known.
  • Names of companies, agencies, insurers, employers, or other parties involved.
  • The injury, loss, denial, workplace issue, civil-rights concern, or consumer problem.
  • Any deadline, government claim, hearing, lawsuit, denial letter, or preservation issue.
  • What proof exists, such as photos, treatment records, wage records, notices, witnesses, or insurance information.

Case review FAQ

Answers before you send the form.

These points help keep the first contact useful, safe, and focused on whether the matter can move.

How much does a case review cost?

The initial case review is free. If the firm accepts a matter, fees, costs, and scope are governed by a written agreement.

What makes a matter a strong contingency fit?

Strong reviews usually have a clear deadline, responsible party, proof, meaningful harm or loss, and a practical recovery source such as insurance, assets, a policy, or another collectible route.

What should I include in the first message?

Include the date, location, people or companies involved, what happened, injuries or losses, known deadlines, and what proof exists. Keep it short and conflict-safe.

Can I send documents or photos?

List the documents or photos that exist, but do not upload sensitive records through the first public form. The firm can request specific records after the initial screen.

What if I am not sure whether my case fits?

Send the essentials. The firm screens for fit, deadlines, proof, damages, and recovery source, then identifies whether the matter may be accepted, needs documents, should be referred, or is not a fit.

What if a deadline is close?

If a government claim window, filing deadline, hearing, denial letter, evidence issue, or other time-sensitive event is close, call instead of waiting on a form submission.
