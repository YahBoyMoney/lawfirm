Frequently asked

Consumer Protection and Lemon Law questions.

What records matter most in a defective vehicle claim?

Every repair order, including the ones that say no problem was found, plus the purchase or lease contract, the warranty booklet, finance documents, and a list of the dates the item was out of service. Ask the service department for printed copies each visit.

The dealer says the problem cannot be reproduced. Does that end the claim?

Not automatically. A repair attempt that resolves nothing is still a documented attempt. Keep your own log of dates, symptoms, mileage, and who you spoke with, and photograph or record the symptom when it appears if it is safe to do so.

Do I have to keep making payments?

Stopping payment can create separate financial and credit consequences that are not part of the underlying dispute. Do not treat a website as advice on your specific contract. Bring the finance documents so the practical options can be discussed.
