When this may fit

Start with a fact pattern, not a conclusion.

A page cannot decide whether a claim exists. These are the kinds of facts that help the firm decide whether a closer review is appropriate.

The injury creates major, lasting, or complex medical and life consequences

A responsible person or organization and liability theory can be investigated

Coverage, assets, or another realistic recovery source may exist

Evidence to preserve

Keep the records that make the chronology testable.

Preserve the original, note where it came from, and keep a simple date index. Do not send sensitive records through a public form.
