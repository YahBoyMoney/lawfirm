# BerheLaw Opus 5 Motion, Conversion, and SEO Rebuild

## Goal
Rebuild BerheLaw as a premium, modern California legal website with restrained scroll motion, stronger lead conversion, and a useful legal-resource architecture while preserving verified facts, intake contracts, legal disclaimers, accessibility, performance, and existing canonical routes.

## Workstreams
1. Baseline inventory and regression lock.
2. Opus 5 creative direction and motion system.
3. Client-centered conversion copy and CTA hierarchy.
4. Homepage and shared component implementation.
5. Legal-resource and practice-area SEO expansion.
6. Accessibility, performance, reduced-motion, browser, and form QA.
7. Independent review, pull request, production verification, and indexing.

## Non-negotiable gates
- No fabricated testimonials, outcomes, awards, offices, attorneys, credentials, response-time promises, or guarantees.
- No fake personnel imagery or stock courthouse/handshake tropes.
- Preserve DBA disclosure, public form action/fields, conflict warning, no-attorney-client-relationship language, sitemap parity, incident citations, and noindex/no-store behavior.
- Motion must fail open and respect `prefers-reduced-motion`.
- Primary call/case-review action visible in the first mobile viewport.
- No horizontal overflow, console errors, broken links, or inaccessible controls.
- Static build remains deterministic and content-hashed.
- Deploy only after tests, cross-browser screenshots, visual inspection, and independent legal-marketing/code review pass.
